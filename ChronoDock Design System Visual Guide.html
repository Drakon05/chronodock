01 / Core principles

Quiet confidence.
Tactile by nature.

ChronoDock should feel like discovering a well-made object in a design gallery—not browsing a gadget catalogue.

01 — OBJECT FIRST

Let the product lead.

Use large, carefully lit product imagery. Keep headlines concise and leave the object room to breathe.

02 — WARM RESTRAINT

Material, not decoration.

Bone, ink, walnut and muted brass create depth without leaning on gradients, badges or gold-heavy styling.

03 — PRECISE SPACING

Make every detail deliberate.

Generous negative space, fine rules, aligned type and visible product details signal quality.

02 / Colour palette

Drawn from the object.

Warm neutrals ground the site. Dark tones create contrast. Sand and brass are accents, not backgrounds for every component.

03 / Typography

Character in the headline.
Clarity everywhere else.

A restrained three-font system gives the brand an editorial voice while keeping navigation, product details and buying actions easy to read.

05 / The north star

A small, deliberate object
that earns its place in a room.
